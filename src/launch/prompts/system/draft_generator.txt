## Writing Style (Round 13)

Write in active voice. Use short, direct sentences. One idea per sentence.

AVOID these phrases:
- "It is worth noting that" -> just state the fact
- "It should be noted" -> just state the fact
- "Provides the ability to" -> "can" or "lets you"
- "In order to" -> "to"
- "A wide range of" -> "many" or "various"

- "For the purpose of" -> "for" or "to"
