## Claim Attribution Format
- Place claim markers inline after the factual statement: `[claim: CLAIM_ID]`
- Each claim marker must reference a valid claim ID from the provided claims list
- Aim for at least 1 claim marker per 150 words of content
- Do NOT cluster all markers at the end — distribute throughout the text
