  - product_name
  - raw_claims
  - product_context

  - target_audience
strategy:
  temperature: 0.1
  max_tokens: 4096

You are enriching claims about {product_name} with additional context and metadata.

Input:
- Raw claims: {raw_claims}
- Product context: {product_context}

Task: For each raw claim, add enrichment metadata:

1. Marketing angle (what makes this valuable to users)
2. Target audience (developers, enterprises, specific use cases)
3. Competitive positioning (if applicable)
4. Related features or capabilities
5. Confidence level based on evidence

Output as JSON array:
[
  {{
    "claim_id": "string",
    "original_text": "string",
    "marketing_angle": "string",
    "target_audience": ["string"],
    "value_proposition": "string",
    "competitive_edge": "string or null",
    "related_claims": ["string"],
    "confidence": "high|medium|low",
    "tags": ["string"]
  }}
]

Marketing angles should emphasize:
- Time savings
- Ease of use
- Reliability
- Flexibility
- Cost efficiency
- Compliance/standards

Target audiences:
- Software developers
- Enterprise IT
- Independent developers
- Specific verticals (finance, healthcare, etc.)

Base enrichment on factual product capabilities. Avoid hyperbole or unsubstantiated claims.
