## Objective

Fix broken TF-IDF similarity imports in `topic_discovery.py` and `chunk_sources.py`. Both files import `tfidf_cosine_similarity` from `launch.workers._shared.embeddings`, which does not exist. The actual function is `compute_tfidf_similarity` in `launch.workers.w2_facts_builder.embeddings`. The broken imports cause silent quality regression: topic dedup is disabled and chunk retrieval falls back to positional ordering.

- specs/03_product_facts_and_evidence.md (Evidence mapping and similarity)
